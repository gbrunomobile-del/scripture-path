Privacy Policy

Manna: Daily Word — Last updated August 27, 2026

Manna: Daily Word ("the App") is operated by Giancarlo Bruno ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you register, we collect your name and email address.

Reading progress: We store your daily reading progress, completed days, streak count and XP points to personalise your experience across sessions.

Usage data: We may collect anonymous usage data (screens visited, features used) to improve the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To save and sync your reading progress
  • To generate AI comprehension questions from the Bible passages you read
  • To improve the App

3. Third-Party Services

Supabase: We use Supabase to store your account and progress data securely. Data is stored on servers in South Africa (af-south-1). See Supabase Privacy Policy.

Anthropic: Bible passage questions are generated using the Anthropic Claude API. Passage text is sent to Anthropic's servers for question generation. See Anthropic Privacy Policy.

Bible API: Bible text is fetched from bible-api.com. No personal data is sent.

4. Data Retention

Your account and reading progress data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

5. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Database access is protected by row-level security policies ensuring you can only access your own data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the address below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
